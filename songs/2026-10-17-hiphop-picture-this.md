Gravelly preacher-toned male rap lead, punchlines doubled an octave up, smooth soulful sung hook. Jazz rap with boom bap drums, 88 BPM. Sonic signature: dusty muted-trumpet and upright-bass loop with a camera-shutter click on beat 4. Drums: dry knocking kick, cracking rimshot snare, sitting slightly behind the beat. Bass: warm round upright walking, deep sub 808 under the hook. Rhodes piano with jazzy ninth chords, brushed ride cymbal, vinyl crackle. 88 BPM · D Dorian · 4/4 · laid-back swing, vocals behind the beat

FLOW BREAKDOWN:
Verse 1: behind the beat, long narrative lines in bars 1-8, then tighter and faster from bar 9 ("Who is that?") into the camera wordplay run.
Bars 15-16: the beat drops out; "Every shot I take is canon" lands dry, then everything returns on the pre-chorus.
Verse 2: half-time for 4 bars (the "Cut to / Wide shot / Close-up" camera directions), then held double-time for 12 bars with rolling three-syllable groups.
Hook: sung, long vowels on "glow / though / slow", 8 to 10 syllables per line against 16 to 20 in the verses.

RAP LAB SETTINGS USED:
Style: Jazz Improv Rap | Flow: behind-beat+triplet | Rhyme: multi-syllabic+mosaic | Density: dense | Vocab: braggadocio+cinematic-storyteller | Persona: first-person-raw

DIRECTOR NOTES:
1. Verse 1 is call-and-response ("Who is that?" asked, answered, then "That's who" at the end of verse 2); verse 2 is bebop runs; the bridge is modal, circling one word, "watch".
2. Leave the trumpet loop slightly dusty and mono in the verses, then open it to stereo with the organ pad on every hook so the sung sections feel like daylight.
3. Rap the verses gritty and close; sing the hook warm and smooth. The bridge is a near-whisper with no beat.
4. In Suno, keep the [Beat Drops Out] and [Stop] tags on their own lines; if the hook comes out rapped, add "sung chorus" near the start of the style prompt.
5. The camera vocabulary carries the whole song: canvas, negatives, shutter, lens, shots, frame. The title works three ways: imagine this, take this photo, and watch this movie.

WORDPLAY MAP (for you, not for Suno):
- canvas: the ring floor / a painting surface
- negative, develops, positive: critics / film photography
- shutter / shudder, lens / lend: sound-alikes
- cannon / canon / Canon: the punch, the history books, the camera brand. Every "shot": a punch, a photo, a chance
- frame it: hang the photo / they framed him as finished
- pupil: a student / the eye widening
- taut / taught: the ring ropes / how he learned
- lesson / lessen
- sole / soul / Seoul: tape on his shoe, his spirit, and the 1988 Seoul Olympics, where a famous boxing decision was stolen
- the hook: the sung chorus / the punch he teaches
- final chorus: "I showed 'em" becomes "they showed 'em", meaning the students now teach by example
- bookend: the first line (the ref counts ten over him) returns as the last line (now he's the one counting ten for his fighters)
