How the AI lyrics generator works

Most AI lyric generators produce lines that rhyme but go nowhere. SONIQ writes lyrics the way a songwriter does, using the same engine as the full SONIQ studio:

  1. A verse with a scene. Not "I miss you", but the coffee stain on the bench seat. Specific details are what make a verse feel true.
  2. A pre-chorus that sets up the hook. Its last line asks a question or makes a promise the chorus answers. That handoff is where songs get their lift.
  3. A chorus built to be sung back. Shorter lines than the verse, the title in the first or last line, and one line sung straight to "you".

Each genre changes the rules: country leans on a title twist, R&B stretches words across melody, EDM keeps the topline sparse for the drop. Learn the craft in our guides: how to write a hook, how to write a country song, how to write an R&B song.

Example: country, "selling my late dad's truck"

[Verse 1]
Found the title in the glovebox Tuesday afternoon
Registration's four years old, inspection's overdue
There's a coffee stain on the bench seat where he'd sit
And a screwdriver rolling round the bed he never did fix

[Pre-Chorus]
'Cause once I sign my name
It's really gone

[Chorus]
Somebody else's truck pulling out my drive
Taking all those back roads you and I rode a thousand times
I know it's steel and chrome
But it still feels wrong
Watching it become somebody else's truck
