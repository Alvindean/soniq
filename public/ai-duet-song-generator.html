How it works

  1. Pick a cast. Choose a duet or group preset in SONIQ's Vocal Cast, and add voice notes if you like ("Rapper A deep and slow, Rapper B high and fast").
  2. Write the song. SONIQ assigns verses, hooks and trades to each performer and writes each one in their own voice.
  3. Paste into Suno. Each section header names its performer with gender and delivery tags, and the style prompt describes every voice.

What you get

  • Section-by-section performer tags Suno follows
  • Voice switches every 2+ lines, never mid-line
  • Shared lines both singers can mean
  • A pre-hook handoff from one voice to the other
  • A style prompt naming every voice

Why duets break in Suno, and how SONIQ fixes it

Suno has no separate vocal tracks. It switches voices from section tags and the style prompt, and two voices of the same gender blend unless they are clearly different. SONIQ separates them for you: low versus high register, gravel versus clean tone, laid-back versus fast flow, different ad-libs. Switches happen at section boundaries, and each performer gets lines that only make sense in their mouth.

FAQ

Can Suno make a song with two different singers?

Yes. Suno switches voices from section tags and the style prompt. It is most reliable with a male and female pair; two same-gender voices need clear contrast in pitch, tone and flow, which SONIQ writes in.

Can I have two rappers and a singer?

Yes. SONIQ's Vocal Cast has presets for two rappers, rapper and singer, rapper and two singers, two rappers and a singer, and a sung duet.

What if one section comes out in the wrong voice?

Use Suno v6 section edit on just that section, for example "In the Bridge, only the female singer sings", instead of regenerating the whole song.
