How it works

  1. Pick two songs. Choose Song A and Song B from your SONIQ songs.
  2. Pick a mode. Mashup combines them directly; Influence writes a new song shaped by both.
  3. Make it. Use the new lyrics and style prompt, or paste the v6 mashup request if both songs are in your Suno library.

What you get

  • Merged lyrics with a hook isolation
  • Blend notes on what came from where
  • A style prompt for the hybrid
  • A Suno v6 mashup request

Mashup or influence?

Mashup keeps recognisable parts of both songs. Influence writes something new that carries the DNA of both: song A's hook energy with song B's groove. Both keep the Hit Craft rules, so the result is a song, not a collage.

FAQ

Can I mash up other artists' songs?

SONIQ works with your own songs, and Suno v6 mashups only work on songs in your own Suno library.

What is the v6 mashup request?

A plain-language instruction for Suno v6, like "vocals and hook from song A over the drums of song B", that you paste into v6.
