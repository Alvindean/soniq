What makes a Suno v6 prompt work

1. Order matters

Suno weighs the start of the style prompt most. Put the genre and the one sound the song is built on first, and the mood words last.

2. Stay under the limit

Prompts past about 800 characters get cut from the end, which is exactly where the vocal description and the emotional close usually sit. The generator keeps every prompt under that line.

3. Describe, don't name

Suno rejects prompts that name real artists. Describe the sound instead: "breathy bansuri flute hook", "sliding 808 that bends root to fifth".

4. Set Variety to 0

In v6, Variety above 0 rewrites your prompt. Keep it at 0 so the prompt you wrote is the prompt Suno uses. Read Suno v6 explained for the rest of the new controls.

5. Match your lyric tags

If the prompt promises a drop or a stripped bridge, tag it in the lyrics too, like [Drop] or [Stripped]. The generator gives you tags that match.

Suno prompt generator FAQ

Is the Suno prompt generator free?

Yes. 5 prompts a day, no sign-up.

Does it work with Suno v6?

Yes. Prompts are written for v6: layered, front-loaded, under 800 characters, with Variety set to 0 so v6 does not rewrite them.

Why no artist names?

Suno rejects prompts that name real artists or bands. The generator describes the sound instead, which also gives you more control.

What are the exclude styles?

Sounds you do not want Suno to drift into for your genre, like trap hi-hats on a country song. Paste them into Suno's exclude field.

Can it describe duets or several voices?

Yes. Describe the voices in your idea, like "male rapper and female singer", and the prompt will name both. SONIQ's Vocal Cast also tags each section with its performer.
